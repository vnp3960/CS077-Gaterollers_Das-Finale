The Story of Duke

Born from a collaborative design session in Settaria, Pilot Cat blends classic side-scrolling arcade flight with high-stakes smuggling runs.

Duke, known across the skies as Pilot Cat, trained under the legendary ace "Trigger" from Strangereal. Operating out of Catlandia, Duke pilots his custom vintage biplane—the Viridescent Claw—across the treacherous Catlantic into Catmerica to deliver precious Cat Nip.

Relentlessly pursued by the Watch Dogs Order and the International Cat Police, Duke relies on razor-sharp aerial maneuvers, quick reflexes, and sheer feline grit to survive every run.

Secret Identity: Although branded an outlaw by international authorities, Duke anonymously funds local orphanages across Settaria with every successful run.

Gameplay Features

Built in Unity with responsive multi-axis flight physics and dynamic hazards.

✈️ Multi-Axis Flight & Banking

Smooth horizontal and vertical movement paired with dynamic aircraft tilt and continuous propeller animation.

💥 Aerial Combat & Hazards

Fire projectiles with custom particle effects to fend off Watch Dogs Order patrols and incoming obstacles.

🌿 Cat Nip Runs & Missions

Collect Cat Nip across scrolling ocean and sky environments while progressing through branching mission states.

💾 Persistent Saves & Settings

Save your run progress and customize audio/control settings that persist safely between play sessions.

🎒 Structured Inventory

Manage collected Cat Nip and mission items with clear capacity rules and item inspection.

🌊 Living World of Settaria

Multi-layered parallax scrolling skies, ocean waves, and hand-crafted world lore.

Controls & Accessibility

Pilot Cat is designed to be straightforward to pick up and readable across screen sizes.

Action Input
Move Aircraft (Up / Down / Left / Right) W A S D or Arrow Keys
Fire Projectiles Spacebar
Pause / Settings Esc

♿ Accessibility Notes

• Keyboard-Only Playable: Full flight, combat, and menu navigation supported without requiring rapid mouse precision.

• High-Contrast UI & Web Page: Built with semantic HTML, keyboard skip-links, clear focus outlines, and readable contrast ratios.

• Persistent Audio & Control Settings: Volume and gameplay preferences save automatically across sessions.
